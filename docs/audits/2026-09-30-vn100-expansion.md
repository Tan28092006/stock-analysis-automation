that merely increase costs. Activity and profitability must therefore be separate
acceptance dimensions. A numerical monthly floor is awaiting the user's choice;
no floor is retrospectively fitted to these results or treated as a forced-buy
quota. Count funded flat-to-long entries separately from adding to held symbols,
and expose first-session portfolio construction rather than treating it as a

same-day/future volume. Baseline momentum has seven fills above the predeclared

with maximum participation 4.26%. This is a warning calculation, **not an
enforced order-size cap**, and daily ADV does not establish auction liquidity,
available counterparty volume, market impact or executable fills. Smaller
capital changes lot rounding and selection; do not linearly claim these are
the outcomes of an unspecified small real-money account.

### Statistical and deployment decision

H1 has 119 sessions, below the locked 252-session inference minimum. The paired
20/40-session block-bootstrap diagnostics use an 88-comparison family; they do
not authorize promotion. All inspected periods remain development data. This
experiment rejects *simply expanding the existing rules as an H1 improvement*;
it does not establish that every possible VN100 strategy must fail.

**Retain VN100 as research data, not as the live scanner's new universe.**
Potential liquidity-aware sizing or ranking hypotheses must be registered
separately before another trial, not silently fitted to SJS/SBT after inspecting
these losses. Full historical membership, corporate-action/reference-price
validation, execution parity and prospective evidence remain required.
