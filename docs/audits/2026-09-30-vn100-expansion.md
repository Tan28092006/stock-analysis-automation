## Implemented bounded membership input

`configs/research/vn100_membership_2026_h1.json` now expresses the July anchor
and February/May changes through 2026-06-30, using the existing causal Timeline
reader. It has 100 members at each date and a 104-symbol union. Availability is
conservatively the next day's close; notably the May notice mirrored at 18:03
on May 7 is not usable at the May 7 close. The input is research-only and does
not certify absence of unlocated interim changes or membership after June.

Eight tests were run RED before adding the input, then GREEN. They verify
January's subsequently removed names, announcement/effective-date separation,
the off-cycle DGC/BAF replacement without double-adding BSR, boundary rejection,
source hashes, and VN30 being a subset on every H1 trading date. The same tests
are added to the research CI contract job. Local tests are not remote CI proof.
