# Research sources and falsifiable hypotheses — 2026-09-30

These sources motivate tests, not profitable-trading claims. Historical data
already inspected is development evidence. No winner-selected rule is deployed.

## Papers → tests

| Primary research | Hypothesis / registered comparison | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Daniel & Moskowitz, Momentum Crashes](https://kentdaniel.net/papers/published/jfe_16.pdf) | Own-portfolio risk scaling; market trend filter; panic/rebound slices | Original crash mechanism includes long-short portfolios; this system is long-only |
